## See the week. Focus on today.

![Weekly grid, day-by-day view and today on a real phone.](media/v0.1.1/week-and-today.jpg)

Choose a weekly grid, a day-by-day week view or today's classes. A compact header keeps timetable selection, the week and more actions within reach. Tap a course for details and editing.

Completed classes are dimmed. Weekend classes remain visible normally; holidays and makeup dates can be configured separately. Dimming is a visual cue, not cancellation of a class or its reminder.

## See the whole semester.

See which weeks each course runs, including alternate weeks and courses that start or end midway through the semester. Open a course calendar to find specific dates, times and rooms.

![Semester overview with teaching-week blocks and a course calendar.](media/v0.1.1/semester.jpg)

Open **Timetable → More → Semester schedule**.

## One glance, without opening the app.

![Four real vivo home-screen widgets: compact today, today, two days and weekly grid.](media/v0.1.1/widgets.jpg)

- **Compact today:** a small space for today's classes.
- **Today:** a wider list of courses, times and rooms.
- **Two days:** today and tomorrow side by side.
- **Week:** a weekly grid, with scrolling for later periods.

Assign a timetable, hide its name, and choose the weekly widget's course-title position independently. The weekly grid supports color bars and filled cards. Actual size and visible content depend on the launcher; some launchers require manual widget placement.

## Your timetable. Your colors.

Light and dark themes, soft filled cards or simple color bars. Position course titles at the top or center. Use wallpaper-based dynamic colors on Android 12+, a built-in palette, or your own theme.

## A background that feels like you.

Pick an image and adjust its strength, blur, saturation and crop position. Keep the atmosphere, while keeping your classes readable.

![Original dawn and aurora gradients applied through the app's actual background settings.](media/v0.1.1/background.jpg)

Open **My → Appearance & theme → Timetable background**. These are custom backgrounds, not a liquid-glass mode.

## Thoughtful everyday tools.

| What you need | What Classy offers |
| --- | --- |
| Bring in your timetable | Import from supported academic systems, files or text, with a preview before confirmation |
| Get the latest schedule | Refresh from your school, preserve your daily time schedule and review changes and local edits |
| Adjust classes | Edit courses, make temporary date changes and manage reusable daily time schedules |
| Prepare for the day | Before-class reminders and daily summaries, subject to system permissions and battery restrictions |
| Keep a copy | Export courses and time schedules for backup, sharing or another device |

## Your time. Your data.

- **No ads or advertising/analytics SDKs.**
- **Local-first:** everyday timetable viewing works offline. Academic import, holiday data and update checks use the network.
- **Academic-system parsing runs on your device after school login**, without a Classy relay server. Compatibility depends on the school's system.
- **Import and export:** formats include Classy, ICS, CSV, WakeUp text/JSON and HTML. See the [guide](GUIDE.zh-CN.md#导入与导出) for details.

## Take your timetable with you.

[**Download the latest Android release ↗**](https://github.com/IMSX3D/Classy/releases/latest)

Requires Android 8.0 or later. Most phones and tablets should use **app-arm64-v8a-release.apk**. Other architectures are available on Releases.

An official version with the same signing identity can be upgraded in place. Export a backup first. Preview and official builds are separate apps; export and import to transfer timetables. **My → About → Get update** downloads an update and opens the system installer for confirmation.

## Built in the open.

Classy is a [GPL-3.0](../LICENSE) fork of [lingion/sleepy](https://github.com/lingion/sleepy), with redesigned UI, themes and widgets. Its timetable foundation and academic-system parsers build on the upstream project.

Thanks to [lingion](https://github.com/lingion), [Nevodev](https://github.com/Nevodev) for [Cresto](https://github.com/Nevodev/Cresto) / [Pear Wall](https://github.com/Nevodev/Pear-Wall), and [Kyant0](https://github.com/kyant0) for [shapes](https://github.com/kyant0/shapes) / [backdrop](https://github.com/kyant0/backdrop). Some academic-system parsing algorithms and school URL data derive from [WakeupSchedule_BUPT](https://github.com/dIT8Zv/WakeupSchedule_BUPT). These UI and parser dependencies are credited under their respective Apache-2.0 licences; see the in-app licence page for the full list.

For local development, use JDK 17+ and Android SDK 37; see the [build guide](GUIDE.zh-CN.md#从源码构建). Official Release builds require configured signing credentials; they do not silently fall back to debug signing.
